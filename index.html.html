Recognize the problem

Need to understand why a component is failing, or how it will perform?

Most FEA projects start with one of these engineering concerns.

01

High stress or excessive deformation

Stress concentration, deformation, yielding, buckling or an uncertain load path.

02

Fatigue or cracking

Repeated loading, cyclic pressure, thermal cycling or an existing crack that needs assessment.

03

Excessive vibration

Resonance, dynamic loads, natural frequencies or vibration-induced fatigue.

04

Damaged equipment

Dents, thinning, cracks, corrosion or deformation affecting continued operation.

05

Design validation

Need to validate a component or equipment design before fabrication.

06

Continued-service decision

Need engineering evidence to determine whether an existing asset can keep operating.

Mechanical FEA services

Choose the engineering behaviour you need to understand

Four core analysis areas — each configured around your actual geometry, loads and acceptance criteria.

01 — Stress Analysis

Stress, strain, deformation & load-path evaluation

Linear and nonlinear FEA for stress, strain, deformation, buckling and load-path evaluation.

  • Pressure equipment
  • Nozzles
  • Skids & frames
  • Manifolds & valves
  • Critical components
02 — Fatigue & Fracture Analysis

Fatigue life, crack growth & fracture risk

Assessment of fatigue life, cyclic damage, crack growth and fracture risk based on project-specific loading and component condition.

  • Pressure vessels
  • Heat exchangers
  • Welded joints
  • Piping components
  • Existing cracks
03 — Vibration Analysis

Modal response, resonance & dynamic stress

Assessment of modal behaviour, resonance, dynamic stress and vibration response.

  • Rotating equipment
  • Machinery
  • Equipment supports
  • Piping
  • Manifolds
04 — Fitness-for-Service

API 579-1 / ASME FFS-1 assessment

Engineering assessment of damaged or degraded components using appropriate API 579-1 / ASME FFS-1 methods and Level 3 FEA where required.

  • Dents
  • Metal loss
  • Local thinning
  • Cracks
  • Distortion
